## Architectural Note for Portfolio Verification
Automated policy validation checks ensure strict syntax compliance, grammar formatting, and baseline security best practices. However, **passing an offline analyzer check does not prove that a policy implements business-appropriate least privilege**. Policy restrictions must be manually reviewed to confirm they match specific team roles and minimize data exposure boundaries.
