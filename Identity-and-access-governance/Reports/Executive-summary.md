# Executive Identity Governance Summary

## Overall Conclusion
Velora has centralized workforce identity and MFA for privileged users, but identity governance remains manual and incomplete. The sample identified an enabled former-user account, expired guests, incomplete MFA enforcement, service-identity control gaps, retained mover access, a Finance approval conflict, stale identities, and unreviewed standing privilege.

---

## Business Impact
Identity is a shared dependency for employee productivity, customer support, development, cloud administration, and finance. A lifecycle or privilege failure can therefore affect several services rather than one application.

---

## Decisions Required
1.  **Approve immediate leaver and guest removal:** Establish hard-disabled flags for active departed users and expired external contractor profiles within a 24-hour window.
2.  **Authorize ownership mandates:** Approve assigned owners, action parameters, and target deadlines for service accounts, internal movers, and separation-of-duties corrections.
3.  **Complete the planned workforce MFA rollout:** Sign off on the conditional access policy schedule to achieve 100% employee enforcement by 15 September 2026.
4.  **Enforce recurring audits:** Mandate a structural shift to quarterly privileged and application-access reviews.
5.  **Formalize lifecycle engineering:** Approve the deployment of a formal HR-to-IT joiner-mover-leaver (JML) workflow.

---

## Success Measures
*   **100%** of active workforce identities under central MFA enforcement.
*   **100%** of sampled leavers disabled on time.
*   **100%** of guests have a named sponsor and a future end date or are disabled.
*   **100%** of privileged identities reviewed quarterly.
*   **100%** of service identities mapped to human owners with interactive sign-in blocked.
