# Scope and Assumptions

---


## Assessment Objective
Identify the most important cybersecurity risks to Velora Commerce GmbH and create a prioritized 90-day improvement plan.


---


## In Scope
- Microsoft 365 and Entra ID identities
- Windows employee endpoints
- GitHub repositories
- AWS production services
- Ubuntu application server
- Customer web application
- Customer and employee data
- 7 named critical vendors
- Security logging, incident response, and backups


---


## Out of Scope
- Physical penetration testing
- Real vendor assessment
- Production vulnerability scanning
- Social engineering
- Legal certification
- Financial audit
- Processing real personal data


---


## Assessment Period
One simulated point-in-time assessment completed in 1 week.


---


## Assumptions
- Asset and control facts come from the fictional company dossier.
- Existing SOC lab work represents evidence of the analyst's technical ability, not Velora's production environment.
- No conclusion is a formal audit opinion or certification.
- Risk scores are qualitative portfolio estimates and require business-owner validation in a real organization.

---


## Safety and Ethics
No real organization, production target, employee, customer, or vendor is tested.

---

## Simulated Risk Appetite

- Low risks may be accepted by the responsible manager.
- Medium risks require a documented owner and review date.
- High risks require a planned mitigation and target date.
- Critical risks require immediate executive review and an interim control within 14 days.
- Any risk involving unrestricted public exposure of customer or employee data is outside normal appetite.
- Any risk capable of stopping checkout for more than four hours is outside normal appetite.
